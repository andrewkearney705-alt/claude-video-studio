# Reel elements: how every reel gets broken down

Every reel the system studies (the nightly Reel Scout, a reel Andrew sends, one of his own posts) is split into the same 14 parts, so reels can be compared part by part over time. Each part gets: **what it is** (plain description), **measured** (numbers where possible), and **role** (did this part likely drive the result, help, or not matter).

| # | Element | What to record | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Concept** | The one idea in a sentence; the angle; which emotion it trades on (curiosity, aspiration, outrage, relief, belonging) | — |
| 2 | **Hook** | First 3 s: first frame, first spoken line, on-screen text, first cut; hook type (result-first, question, contrarian, number, story, trend sound, pattern-break visual) | time to first cut, words in first line |
| 3 | **Script structure** | Beat outline (hook → setup → turn → payoff → CTA); sentence shape (length, "you" vs "I", open loops, lists of 3); payoff position | words/min, avg sentence length, seconds to payoff |
| 4 | **Talent** | Who's on camera, energy, delivery (to camera, voiceover, no talking), eye line, wardrobe, credibility signal | — |
| 5 | **Camera** | Who's filming (selfie, tripod, second shooter), lens feel (wide, tight, 0.5x), movement, framing | — |
| 6 | **A-roll** | The main shot: what it is, how long each take holds | median shot length |
| 7 | **B-roll** | What cutaways show (proof, process, mood, screen recordings, stock, AI-made); how often | B-roll share of runtime, cutaways/min |
| 8 | **Setting** | Where it's shot; props that tell you who this is; how often the location changes | — |
| 9 | **Lighting and grade** | Key light, contrast, warmth, black level, saturation, letterbox | luminance p2/p50/p98, saturation |
| 10 | **Audio (voice)** | Mic quality, room sound, pace, pauses | dead air > 0.15 s |
| 11 | **Music and SFX** | Bed type, trending sound or not, SFX count and kinds, ducking, music drops | SFX/min, bed level vs voice |
| 12 | **Editing** | Cuts/min, jump cuts, zooms, layout changes (full, split, letterbox), transitions | cuts/min, layout changes/min |
| 13 | **Text and graphics** | Caption style (size, words at a time, position), title cards, stickers, animation | words per caption |
| 14 | **CTA and funnel** | What it asks (follow, comment keyword, save, share), where it leads (DM, email, long-form) | — |

**Plus the result**, always next to the breakdown:
- Views, likes, comments, shares/reposts (whatever is visible), post date, and **outlier score** = this reel's views ÷ the creator's median of their last 12 reels.
- **Engagement shape:** comments per 1K likes, reposts per 1K likes, saves if visible. High comments = conversation or keyword CTA; high reposts = identity or pledge content; high likes, low comments = mood or aesthetic.
- **Comment read:** top 20 comments sorted into what people respond to (the idea, the person, the craft, the CTA keyword, disagreement, "me too"). Quote nothing longer than a few words.

## Finding "why it worked"
Never explain a hit from the hit alone. For each outlier, also break down **one normal reel from the same creator** (near their median views). The elements that differ between the two are the likely causes; the elements that are the same are just the creator's style. Write the verdict as: "Likely drivers: <elements>, because <difference>. Not drivers: <elements> (same in their normal posts)." Say how sure you are (low, medium, high).
