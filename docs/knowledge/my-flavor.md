## His toolkit picks (from the Content Vault strategies)
Adopted (reach for these first when they fit):
- Dark warm set: low-key room, warm skin, practical lamps or cool monitors behind, shallow depth of field (= look B).
- One accent colour carries the frame; his is one clay accent.

Liked (shortlist, try per reel; not defaults):
- Grade: colour talking head, near-mono B-roll and titles; lifted filmic blacks ~10-20, saturation 0.25-0.33, ambers over blue.
- Cutting: zero dead air (sounds like one take); picture changes every 1.5-2 s while he keeps talking; ~1.25x punch-in only on key lines, never under a card.
- Text: captions and labels hard-cut on the exact syllable, no fades or bounces.
- Cold open: ~1 s trailer-style title sequence inside the first 3 s (see `formats/curiosity-essay.md`).
- Sound: dense hits (riser into the hook, whoosh per cut, ticks on captions). Busier than the 10-16 a minute the Editor suggested and the ~24 measured in `look-and-sound.md` §3b; test before changing defaults.
- AI characters: a plain white reference sheet (face close-up, full body front and back, neutral light), show the model the location, always describe camera, lens and light.

## Remix recipe (run this for every "make one like that")
1. **Pull the drivers** from the reference's breakdown (`elements.md`). Those are the only parts carried over, and only in structure (e.g. "trend-sound supercut hook," "result shown in frame 1"), never their words, footage or likeness.
2. **Swap the content**: his real project, footage or story, from the board or the Footage Scout.
3. **Apply his look** above for everything that wasn't a driver: setting, grade, type, sound.
4. **Check against what's proven for him** (`patterns.md`, "Proven for Andrew"), which outranks anything proven for other creators.
5. **Offer 3 hook versions** so the Trial Lab can test them.
6. Say in one line what was borrowed and what's his.
