# Brand V2: Pine, Oat & Electric

Adopted by Andrew on Nov 4, 2026 (design session). This is the house look for every reel, carousel and page. The full guidelines live on the Brand page of the Brand OS (`meta/brand`); this file is the editing kit's copy of the parts the edit needs.

## The color rule
**Green is you. Blue is the key idea. Mono marks AI and tools.**
- Blue (Electric) is the one line or word to remember, once per video or slide, in any topic. It does not mean "AI."
- AI and tool names (CLAUDE, HIGGSFIELD) go in small Geist Mono labels, never in blue.
- Honey and Oat are fills only, never small text.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Pine | #2F4B3A | Anchor green: name, big fills, "you" moments |
| Fern | #4E6E52 | Everyday green next to the blue |
| Fern light | #A9C4A6 | Green text on dark and on footage |
| Electric | #3D5BFF | Key-idea fills and chips |
| Electric ink | #2F4BE6 | Key idea as small text on light |
| Electric light | #97A9FF | Key idea on dark and on footage |
| Honey | #B98A52 | Warm fills (slides, tags) |
| Oat | #E2D3B8 | Light cards and surfaces |
| Walnut | #5A3E2C | Body text on light, wood tone |
| Cream | #F6EFE3 | Light ground (default) |
| Night | #16110D | Dark ground (walnut-black) |
| Bone | #F2EADC | Text on dark |

Light is the default. Dark for text-heavy reels, night or tech pieces, and big type.

Tested: every text pairing in use passes WCAG 4.5:1. Electric on Cream is big-text only (4.4), so small blue text uses Electric ink (5.6). Pine on Night fails, so dark mode uses Fern light. Pine and Walnut look alike to red-green color-blind viewers: never use them to mean different things.

## Type
- **Satoshi** (Fontshare, free commercial; web stand-in Plus Jakarta Sans): Black for hooks and headlines, Bold for captions, Medium for body.
- **Fraunces Italic** (Google Fonts): the one word that matters inside a sans line. Green by default, Electric when it is the key idea.
- **Geist Mono** (Google Fonts): small letter-spaced caps labels, series tags, slide numbers.

| Role | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hook (reel) | Satoshi Black 96–120px, leading 1.0, 3–5 words, one Fraunces italic word |
| Captions (reel) | Satoshi Bold 54–60px, sentence case, 3–4 word chunks, Bone/white with soft shadow |
| Key idea | Fraunces italic, Electric light on footage, once per video |
| Carousel headline | Satoshi Black 96–120px, -3% tracking, one Fraunces italic word |
| Carousel body | Satoshi Medium 40–46px, max 22 words per slide |
| Labels | Geist Mono 500, 24–26px, letter-spaced caps |

## Name, not logo
No logo mark. His face, style and name are the brand. Name wordmark: "Andrew" in Satoshi Black + "Kearney" in Fraunces italic (Pine on light, Fern light on dark), small, first 2 seconds only. Never a pinned headline.

## Voice (for on-screen text)
High energy, playful hype; plain words; to skeptics "Fair, and here's proof." Never hustle-bro or forced memes and slang.
