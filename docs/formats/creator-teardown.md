# Creator Teardown (Kallaway style)

Pick a creator who is crushing it, run their channel through Claude, name 3 patterns, show the proof, tag them, give away the method. This is Andrew's core strategy in one format: it teaches, it shows what Claude can do, and the person you study has a reason to repost you.

Read this honestly: a teardown of a smaller creator got about 1/7 the reach of his own named framework, but the highest comment rate. **Teardowns are a lead-gen engine more than a reach engine.** Balance them with "wow" posts that travel further, and pick subjects whose audience overlaps yours (their repost is the reach).

## Measured fingerprint (Dd8vo8IxamX, frame by frame)
- **Length:** 120 s. Three insights, ~25-30 s each, plus a 10 s hook and 15 s close.
- **Layout:** top ~2/3 is a black dot-grid canvas for graphics; bottom ~1/3 is a strip of his face (landscape crop). Full-frame face for the key line of each section, roughly every 8-15 s (3.8, 8, 21, 30, 36, 51, 67, 71, 83, 87, 103, 107, 119 s).
- **Hook (0-4 s):** the subject's own clip shrinks into a phone mockup of their profile; a follower counter runs 10,000 to 500,000; headline bar on top (white bold line + a second line on a red strip). Cut to full-frame face at 3.8 s.
- **Progress system:** a "01 Identity Labels" chip sits top-left on every graphic in that section. Between sections, a list recap (01/02/03 in grey pills) highlights the next item with a red bar.
- **Evidence graphics, never stock:** grid of the subject's thumbnails, red pills with the exact terms they use, an annotated still with callout labels ("lighting", "outside", "wall"), view-count badges ("759K"), a red circle highlight, a grid with the repeated row lit green ("The Redundancy"), a ring of avatars for "works in every niche".
- **Captions:** one word at a time, small, white, centred on the seam between canvas and face strip.
- **Look:** face shots very low-key (median luminance ~27/255, blacks ~9, highlights ~150), black cap and tee, warm key light, dark room with a practical. Graphics sit on pure black.
- **Close:** shows the Claude analysis on screen ("I Deep Analysis Nate's Full Channel" with Claude + Sandcastles logos), then the CTA card over a screenshot of the giveaway: "Comment NATE to get the full guide on analysing any channel". Shoutout to @nategaffney in the caption.

## The three patterns he found (reusable rules for Andrew's own content)
1. **Identity Labels:** name a type of person ("Intellectual Omnivore", "Analytical Empath"). People who see themselves in it engage hard, like a horoscope. Andrew's version: labels for his audience, e.g. "the corporate builder", "AI-native operator", "the one-person studio". Test one per reel.
2. **Series Familiarity:** same visual setup every video (Nate: him, good light, outside, leaning on a wall). Repetition is a feature: viewers see thousands of videos between yours. Andrew: lock one set for the moonshot series and stop changing it.
3. **Demeanor Matching:** delivery should match the audience you want (Nate reads as a vulnerable smart nerd for gifted thinkers). Andrew: curious builder who shows the work, not a guru.

## Beat sheet
1. **0-4 s Hook:** the result in numbers over the subject's real profile ("went from 10K to 500K in 1 month"). Cut to your face by 4 s.
2. **4-10 s Promise:** "These are the 3 non-obvious things he did." Show the 01/02/03 list.
3. **3 sections, ~25 s each:** name (2 words) → what it is → proof on screen → one line on how anyone can use it (full frame face).
4. **Method reveal (~10 s):** show Claude doing the analysis. This is the "how" and the proof you're credible.
5. **CTA:** "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [the method/tool]" over a shot of the giveaway. Tag the subject in caption and on screen.

## Shot list for Andrew
- A-roll: dark room, warm side key, black or dark top, one practical behind. Shoot vertical 4K; the edit crops a strip for the bottom third and uses the full frame for key lines.
- Screen recordings: the subject's profile scrolling, their top reels, and the Claude analysis (the contact sheets / findings).
- Stills: 6-12 of the subject's thumbnails with view counts (screenshot their grid).

## Edit recipe
- Canvas: black with a faint dot grid. Headline bar: white bold sans, second line on a red strip.
- Section chip top-left on every graphic; list recap between sections.
- One accent colour (Kallaway: red; Andrew should pick his own and keep it).
- Every claim gets a proof graphic built from the subject's real content.
- Sound: low bed, soft click on each chip/pill, whoosh on full-frame cuts.
