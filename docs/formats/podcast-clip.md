# Podcast / Interview Clip (Diary of a CEO style)

Built from 11 Diary of a CEO reels (143K–1.4M views each, Oct 2026). DOAC runs one template on every clip, and it works.

## Measured fingerprint
| | Target | DOAC measured |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 60–95s (up to 130s if the story holds) | 62–129s |
| Hook frame held | 5–6s before the first cut | first cut at 5–9s in 7 of 10 |
| Cuts per minute after the hook | 10–22 | 8–29 |
| Median shot | 2–3.5s | 1.7–3.6s in 9 of 10 (calmer than Roberto) |
| Pauses | trimmed, but some breathing room OK | 0–6 per minute |

## The template
1. **Hook frame (0–5s):** stack two shots: host reacting on top, guest speaking on bottom. Over the seam, a **white rounded box with a black, all-caps question**, 3–6 words, ending in ? or ...:
   - "THE WORLD ORDER IS COLLAPSING..." (1.4M)
   - "WORLD WAR 3 IS COMING?" (881K)
   - "IS TRUMP LYING ABOUT THIS?" (579K)
   - "IS THIS THE BEST INVESTMENT EVER?" (238K)
   A small credential label next to the guest ("WWIII Threat Professor"). Show logo badge top-left the whole clip.
2. **Body:** cut to full-screen single-camera shots of the guest, with host reaction close-ups every few lines.
3. **Captions:** one word at a time, white, centered low; emotional or key words in yellow.
4. **B-roll inserts:** motion-graphic maps, a globe, data charts with one giant number (+4.5%), document scans, archival photos. Each insert is 1.5–3s and lands on the word it illustrates.
5. **End:** on the guest's strongest line, no outro.

## Picking the moment
Scan the transcript for: a prediction, a number, a contradiction of common belief, a personal confession, or a clear "here's how". The clip must make sense with zero context; open on the claim, not the setup.

## Distribution move
Steven posted one clip from his personal account with "Comment 'watch' and I'll DM you the full episode" (789K views, 4,478 comments). Use that on the best clip of each episode.

## Shooting a conversation so it cuts like this
- 3 cameras: wide two-shot, tight on each person. Dark background, practical lights, warm key.
- Mic each person separately. Leave 2s of silence before key answers.

## Look & feel spec (from the frame-by-frame review; full detail in style-bible.md)
- **0–4.5s:** host top (silent skeptical look), guest bottom. White rounded box on the seam with 2 lines of black all-caps condensed bold (Archivo Black), on from frame 1, no animation.
- **~2s:** credential tag next to the guest in small white bold with a tiny hand-drawn curved arrow ("WWIII Threat Professor").
- **Show badge:** top-left on every frame — light box with the show name in black bold, guest name small underneath. Andrew needs his own version.
- **~4.5–5s:** captions start; **~5–6s:** first cut to full-frame guest.
- **Captions:** one word at a time, lowercase, white bold sans, low-centre; emotional words in yellow/orange.
- **Inserts:** dark motion graphics — red/gold lit maps, a globe, scanned documents, charts with one giant number (+4.5%).
- **Tone:** grave and high-stakes; the box is a question or a warning. Post caption: neutral 3–5 sentence summary naming the guest's credential, then "Comment 'watch' and we'll send you this episode."
