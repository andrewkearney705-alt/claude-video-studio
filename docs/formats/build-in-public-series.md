# Build-in-Public / Series / Day-in-the-Life

The fastest-growing creators in the set run **numbered series**: Rajan Chida's "Day 290 — Building Royal Oak Retreat" (542K followers), Chase Ridgeway's "Season 2, Episode 87" (13.1M views), Roberto's "Agent Wars, Episode 02/03" (3.5M, 1.2M). A series gives people a reason to follow, not just watch.

## Three variants
**A. Story arc with money (Chida).** "9 weeks ago I bought 2.5 acres..." (6.9M). Big claim with a number in line 1, then the struggle, then where it stands now. Show money on screen in red ("$11,000"). Whiteboard explainer shots for anything financial ("CONSTRUCTION LOAN" with equity / loan / value boxes).
**B. Relationship hook (Chida).** Two people on camera, a surprising premise: "We dated the same girl" (1.2M), "I just ended a relationship" (his business partner, 591K). The business story hides inside a personal hook.
**C. Day-in-the-life (Chase).** Timestamped segments ("6:30am — trying something new today"), first-person, real job and real places. The 13.1M reel is an ordinary 9-to-5 office day. Episode number in the caption. Brand partners fit in naturally (#BestBuyPartner reel: 6.2M).
**D. Competition series (Roberto Agent Wars).** Same test given to several AI models, physical stakes (3D-printed bridges loaded with weights), a scoreboard graphic, company logos labelling each result. Serif title "Top 5 AI models" with a hand-drawn arrow.

## Script rules
- Line 1: a number, a time, or a confession. "2 years ago I got robbed." "I'm graduating college in a month."
- Every episode must move the story forward one visible step (land bought, cabin framed, first guest).
- End on an open loop for the next episode, or a payoff number.

## Shot list for Andrew
- Walk-in shots: tripod, wide, you walk into frame and start talking (Chida's lecture-hall and cabin shots).
- Selfie vlog shots on location for momentum.
- Whiteboard or screen for any explanation.
- Before/after pairs of the same angle for progress.
- Collaborator on camera when the story has two people.

## Edit recipe
- Small white captions, one short phrase at a time, centered.
- Red or yellow for numbers and money.
- Episode/day tag in the first frame ("Day 1", "Episode 03") as a bold title card.
- Music bed throughout, SFX on reveals, hard cuts on beats.

## What underperformed
Sponsored or trade-show booth content without a story (Chida's booth reel: 30K vs his 591K–6.9M story reels).

## Look & feel spec (from the frame-by-frame review; full detail in style-bible.md)
- **Chida-style story:** 2–3 word lowercase captions in white bold with a thin black outline, placed above your head in the upper third; money in green; one all-caps shout word when the story peaks ("NUMBERS!"); whiteboard handwriting for any maths; drone shot of the place; tripod wides in a found space (an empty lecture hall).
- **Chase-style day-in-the-life:** small native white text; 2-line premise held ~7s; then a time + action label per scene ("4:56pm / Work is over"); static tripod wides you walk through; end on a twist.
- **Agent Wars-style competition:** contestant card top-left (brand bold + logo, model small); custom scoreboard card that returns and updates; big centre counters; one serif word per fast shot in the intro; app-icon stickers popping around you.
- **Episode tag:** "Day 290" / "Episode 03" as a title card in the first frame (Chida uses a black box with script "Day 1" and a yellow condensed subtitle).
- **Tone:** casual, money-transparent, friend energy, self-deprecating.
