# AI / Tech News Explainer

The format behind Roberto Nickson's biggest explainers (Tesla Roadster 6.8M, Gemini 5.7M, OpenAI launch 1.6M) and Rui Fu's AI tutorials (1.3M, 515K). Someone just launched something; you tell people why it matters before anyone else does.

## Measured fingerprint (from 15 reels by rpn and raycfu, Oct 2026)
| | Target | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 50–75s | rpn 52–79s, raycfu 47–60s |
| Cuts per minute | 25–45 | rpn explainers 15–44 (most 31–44); raycfu 13–23 |
| Median shot | 1.0–2.5s | rpn 1.0–2.6s; raycfu 1.8–4.4s |
| Pauses over 0.15s | 0 (max 2 in the whole video) | rpn Genie 4: 2 pauses in 71s |
| First cut | under 1s | rpn 0.25–0.85s |
| Sound hits | 30–50 per minute, music bed never drops | rpn Gemini 53/min |
| Speed to post | same day as the launch | Roberto: a 3-day-late launch video is worthless |

## Structure (beat sheet)
1. **0–2s Hook:** "[Company] just launched [thing], and it changes [X]." Visual: the official launch clip or a striking prop, full screen, title in bold caps.
2. **2–5s Conflict:** why the viewer should care, or what everyone is getting wrong.
3. **5–45s Explain in 3 beats:** what it is, how it works (show it), what it means. Each beat ends on a mini-payoff.
4. **45–60s Bigger picture:** the stakes with a hard number (market size, money raised, a famous quote).
5. **Last 3s Reward / CTA:** a question to comment on ("Have you tried it?"), or Rui Fu's move: "Comment PLUGINS and I'll send you the guide" (his 515K reel drew 13K comments, more than its 8.7K likes).

## Shot list for Andrew
- Talking head at desk: side key light, practical lamp + monitors glowing behind, dynamic handheld mic as a prop. Read each line 2–3 times.
- 1 physical prop that IS the metaphor (Roberto: a CRT TV showing the Google logo; a basketball for a product launch).
- Screen recordings of you using the tool (Screen Studio style smooth zooms).
- Official launch footage of the product (download from the company's own channel).
- AI-generated B-roll for anything that doesn't exist on film (Claude writes the prompts).

## Edit recipe
- **Split-screen as the base layout:** B-roll on top half, you on bottom half, small white captions on the seam.
- Every 10–15s break it: go full-screen on you with one huge word ("GENIE"), or full-screen B-roll, or a serif title card on black ("SIMULATION").
- Data moments: grid montage of clips, a chart, a logo row.
- Captions: 1–2 words, small, white, on the seam; key words in a wide display font full-width.
- Color: dark, moody studio, warm skin.
- Sound: cinematic bed under everything; whoosh on each cut, tick on word reveals, riser into the hook.

## What underperformed
- Horizontal/brand-ad cuts posted as-is (Roberto's Slack ad, 154K, his lowest).
- Info-dumps with no stakes line.

## Look & feel spec (from the frame-by-frame review; full detail in style-bible.md)
- **Layout:** 50/50 split from frame 1 (B-roll top, you bottom). Break it every 10–15s with a full-screen shot or a title card on black.
- **Hook title:** 2 lines over the top half, small line + big line, all caps, extended wide sans (Unbounded/Syncopate). Slides in with a 2–3 frame horizontal smear, smears out on the next shot. If covering a brand, echo the brand's own type (Roberto set "RORDSTER" in Tesla-like lettering).
- **Running captions:** 1–3 words, small all-caps monospace, white, letter-spaced, sitting exactly on the split seam. Hard cut on each spoken word.
- **Keyword promotion:** once every 5–10s one word jumps into a big display font, a different one each time (thin serif "FRONTIER", script "Huge" stacked over condensed "DEAL", serif "SIMULATION"). Stacked words appear one at a time on their spoken beat.
- **Overlays:** rounded screenshots with a small grey pill label ("BENCHMARKS"); a physical prop with content composited onto it (CRT TV showing static → glitching logo); Rui Fu-style gold-keyword serif headline and floating proof cards work too.
- **Tone:** start mid-thought, react ("WHAT?!"), no greeting, end on stakes or a question. Post caption = the facts as bullets.
- **Set:** dark room, warm skin, coloured monitor glow and a practical lamp behind you, handheld mic as a prop.
