## Beat sheet
1. **0-1.4 s** Question to camera: "Why do [group] always [odd thing]?"
2. **1.4-2.4 s** Trailer title: the topic in 2-3 words, built shot by shot over B&W footage.
3. **2.4-6 s** "I'd always wondered, so I dug in" + first proof flash (product macro, then a page of print).
4. **The answer in one line**, then the contrast (them vs us), then one concrete example with a surprising jump (film coatings → screens → cosmetics).
5. **Nuance turn:** give the other side its due in one sentence.
6. **Close:** point to a related video (a friend's or your own long-form) instead of a CTA ask.

## The title sequence (the signature move)
- English word in a heavy stencil/brush display face in **one accent colour**, plus a second-language or symbol layer in white (kanji in the source), then the second word slams in.
- Over desaturated, high-contrast stock (cars, motorcycles, people), one shot per ~0.2 s.
- Camera-viewfinder HUD around it: thin corner brackets, tiny monospace data text, a small "PLAY ▶" marker bottom right.
- 2-3 frame glitch hits between shots: RGB split, horizontal scan bars. Glitch never touches the talking head.
- Sound: a riser into it and a hit on each word (measure when building; audio not measured in the source).

## B-roll recipe (editorial collage)
- Scanned magazine/catalog pages and product cut-outs on white paper, sliding or fanning in as small labelled cards.
- Archival or street footage of the place being discussed, drained to near mono.
- Real product macros (badges, chips, boards) and technical drawings or sketches.
- One physical prop card held up on a key word ("Opposite" playbook in the source).

## Text
- Running captions: 1-2 words, tiny monospace caps in a dark box, lower centre (matches Andrew's adopted small Space Mono caps).
- One display slam per ~20 s on the key idea (bold extended caps, e.g. "ACCUMULATED EXPERTISE"), and the occasional script word on a prop.

## Andrew's version (remix, not a copy)
- Topic: a "why does X work this way" question from his own world (craft, cooking, outdoors, building with Claude, how something is made). Never his employer.
- Set: his light-desk or daylight look; keep the warm colour face vs mono B-roll split.
- Title: Space Mono HUD + his one clay accent instead of red stencil, so it reads refined, not busy (v1 lesson: "a bit much").
- His camera edge: shoot real macros of the objects in the story instead of stock where he can.
- Build path: `short-form-video-studio` with this file as the format; the title sequence is an animation scene (HTML/Remotion), ~1 s, cut into the hook.
