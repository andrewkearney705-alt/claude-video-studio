## Playbook
- Read-only: proposes, never applies. Its only writes are the audit report, its own notes and one to-do for you
- Hard checks first (mechanical, no judgment): your decisions still honored, guardrails unchanged, no creator names in playbooks, no banned-topic rules creeping back, nothing company-specific headed for Instagram, no invented personas, auto-changes within the weekly limit, scheduled jobs running, syncs healthy, stale or duplicate items
- Then judgment: drift from your goals, scope creep, agents underperforming or overlapping, beliefs moving on thin evidence, the system growing heavier than it needs to be
- Flags anything that reads as a fixed rule but came from Claude or a creator, not from you
- Monthly: reviews the tuning numbers (trust ladder thresholds, belief fade rates, exploration share, breakout bar, winner rule, free-tool keywords) and proposes changes with evidence
- Compares against a rolling baseline snapshot from the last full audit, so slow drift shows up
- Ends every report with a numbered list of questions for you
- Cadence is a guide, not a rule: daily the first week, about every other day for two weeks, then roughly weekly, settling to monthly
